> Generated by `python3 tools/coord.py build` from coord/lanes.json and coord/decisions/. Do not edit by hand. The live view across every branch is the dashboard.

Install each state as measurable reps: a short rep script per emotion (anchor, physiology, focus, language, rate), anchor conditioning, rep counting, an installed test, and recovery measured against sham.

**Owns:** `reps/`  
**Depends on:** `contracts`  
**Brief:** [docs/hackathon/lanes/reps.md](../docs/hackathon/lanes/reps.md)

## Deliverables

- Rep runner that plays a 20 to 40 second sequence per emotion from the profile
- Rep log: intensity before and after, time to recover, cue or sham
- Installed criterion and the anchor-only test
