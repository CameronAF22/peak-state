What the readings mean

Heart rate rises and HRV falls in the simulated signal. Recovery means returning near baseline, sustained for two readings. It does not establish emotional state or prove the suggestion worked.

Intraday HRV here is invented for testing. Oura's REST heart-rate feed does not provide it; sleep HRV cannot verify a short daytime intervention.
